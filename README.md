# City of Kraków — Cracow Without Barriers

Initial project scaffold for HackYeah 2026, 3–4 October in Kraków.

Help residents and visitors assess the accessibility of places and routes against their individual needs, using detailed information about barriers and facilities.

## Scope

- Focus the prototype on a defined user group, such as wheelchair users or parents with pushchairs.
- Show concrete barriers, data provenance, update dates, and reliability; distinguish unverified or missing information.
- Use public sources without requiring internal city systems or a city-maintained database.
- Demonstrate accessible interaction, including keyboard access, screen-reader support, and a text alternative to maps.

## Deliverables

- Working prototype or demonstration, target-user description, and data reliability approach.
- Business model, maintenance approach, and plan for scaling to other cities.
- PDF presentation of up to 10 slides and a demonstration video of up to 3 minutes.
- The competition rules specify a Polish submission.
